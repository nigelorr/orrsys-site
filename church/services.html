How can we help?

In short, anything related to audio systems in churches. So, whether you have no sound system in your church, and would like to consider taping or making CDs of services for those who can't attend or fitting an induction loop for the hearing-impaired, or you've got a 24 channel desk and thousands of pounds worth of equipment but no-one is quite sure how to use it to its best advantage, we can help!

Training

No matter how much you have spent on equipment, if the people who operate it don't know what to do, you will not be making the most of your system. We can offer training courses tailored to your system and your church, and most importantly the people who operate it.

These courses can be arranged to suit your situation, anything from an evening course covering the basics of operating your system to a series of evenings or a full day course covering more advanced topics. In either case, participants will get hands-on experience on relevant equipment, and advice on improving their technique

Technical Support

We offer technical support to our customers when they need it. For non-urgent questions, go to our contact page to find out more.

If you have a more general question, you might find it is answered in the resources in our technical pages.

System Upgrades and improvements

Complete Systems

If your installation is not upgradable, or you need a new system for a hall or meeting room, we can design and specify a complete system to meet your needs

Installations

Once you have a system design, we can hand it over to you or your preferred contractor for installation, or we can continue to oversee and support the installation to completion. Whichever you choose, you can rely on us to commission and test the system comprehensively, so that you can get the most out of it for years to come.

Uniquely, Orr Systems will work with volunteers in your congregation to complete the installation, if that is of interest to you. This is an excellent way of either saving money for the same high standard of installation, or being able to get more equipment for the same budget. If you have volunteers who could help, whether they have existing technical skills or not, contact us to find out more!

Prices

Our normal fees are £80 per hour for training courses, consultancy and design. However, we appreciate that every situation is different, so contact us, free of charge and without obligation, for a chat and a quote!
